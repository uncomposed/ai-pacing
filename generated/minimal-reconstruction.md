## Kernel

### K1 — Separate capability, substrate, and power

Algorithmic capability (A), strategic hardware (H), and power-conversion affordances (P) are related but distinct variables and should not be governed as though they always change together.

### K2 — Pace conversion into irreversible power

The objective is to slow and condition the conversion of frontier intelligence into irreversible economic, political, military, and physical power, not to freeze intelligence as such.

### K3 — Govern bounded research and consequential use differently

Research inside genuinely bounded environments may proceed under a different regime from public, commercial, military, governmental, or otherwise power-conferring use.

### K4 — Require adversarial assurance above the public frontier

Consequential use that materially exceeds the observable foreign public capability frontier requires an adversarial control and safety case.

### K5 — Start domestically and remain internationally extensible

The regime should begin as a domestically justified institution that can operate without foreign agreement and later admit deeper reciprocal cooperation.

### K6 — Make foreign public capability an automatic reciprocity input

Observable foreign public capability should alter the domestic safe-harbor frontier so that foreign restraint can be reciprocated without a treaty and foreign release cannot exploit unilateral domestic restraint indefinitely.

### K7 — Maximize warning time

Governance should maximize the interval between credible detection of a dangerous transition and the point at which humans can no longer respond effectively.
